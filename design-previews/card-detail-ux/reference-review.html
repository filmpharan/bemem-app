The references are displayed with a CSS crop of the app viewport only (approximately image y=176–1670). Original JPG files are untouched. No browser address bar, toolbar or Android navigation is recreated in the prototype.

What changed carefully

  • Same scan → card/expiry/flip → member number → History order, header Share on the left and Close on the right. No segmented control or duplicate Close button.
  • Small 36px visual controls have 48×48px web hit areas. Code toggle has a separate bottom lane, outside the mock code's quiet margin.
  • The reference card appears approximately 2:1; this brief explicitly requires 6×4. The sample is therefore taller, with a 14px radius. Portrait 4×6 is also available.
  • Placeholder warning and Coming soon labels are intentional honesty/accessibility additions. None of the graphics can be scanned.
  • The bottom-anchored sheet grows upward in QR mode; on small or enlarged-text displays it scrolls rather than shrinking the code.
